A simple hiring checklist

Before approving plumbing work, take a moment to check the basics:

  • Request the applicable license details and write down the number and name provided.
  • Confirm that the information matches the person or business responsible for the job.
  • Describe the issue and ask what assessment is needed to identify its cause.
  • Request a plain-language description of the proposed work before agreeing to it.
  • Ask which details are still uncertain and what would need to be checked on site.

A common mistake is treating a quick phone description as a confirmed diagnosis. Another is assuming that a business name, online listing, or confident answer proves a credential.
